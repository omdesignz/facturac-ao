# facturac.ao — promo, three storylines

**Status: waiting for a pick. Nothing is built for any of them.**

## The one thing

**Cobre quem lhe deve: o lembrete já está escrito, e só sai quando você confirma.**
Close on the proof that the software is certified (AGT certificate FE/419/AGT/2026, 28 October 2026) and on the deadline (1 January 2027).

**One viewer, one task.** A shop owner (the demo company's "Ana") opens the app to chase the money she is owed. **One real number travels the whole film: 378 000 Kz owed by Mavinga & Filhos, 49 days late** (509 100 Kz owed in total). Every piece of the app on screen is the real app (screens captured from it; labels exactly as they are today). Facts used, all from the product or the certificate:

- "Quem me deve?", 509 100 Kz por receber, the bar showing 74% / 26%.
- The reminder is prepared, in a firm tone for 49 days, with the two overdue invoices listed.
- "Nada é enviado até confirmar." "Enviar 2 lembretes."
- Certificada pela AGT, FE/419/AGT/2026, 28 de outubro de 2026.
- A partir de 1 de janeiro de 2027: Regime Geral e Regime Simplificado emitem factura electrónica.

Nothing else is claimed: no "in seconds", no speed, no savings figure, no customer count.

## Recommended: A — "Os 378 000"

**Device:** the result first. **Form:** one continuous take.
**World:** the real app on a white ground; the camera pulls back and pushes in through it in one unbroken move, no cuts.

- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a number so large it fills the screen, then the camera pulls back and the number turns out to be the line in a list in the app, who owes you, and the film follows that one debt as it is turned into a message and sent. The feeling is relief: the hard conversation is already written.
- **First two seconds, exactly:** frame 0 is "378 000 Kz" at 330 px, ink on white, with "49 dias" under it in red. At 0.8 s the whole frame begins to pull back through the 3 in "378"; by 2.0 s the number has become the first row of "Quem me deve?" on a phone.
- **Beats:** hook (the number, 0–2 s) → tension (509 100 Kz owed, 74% in one company, the bar fills, 2–7 s) → reveal ("Quem me deve?" named as the screen, 7 s) → proof (tap the debtor, the message is already there, change one word, "Enviar 2 lembretes", 7–19 s) → payoff ("Enviado." with the number leaving the list, 19–22 s) → claim ("Cobrar, sem a conversa difícil.") → end card (the .ao dot becomes the shield: Certificada pela AGT, certificate number; then "Abrir conta grátis", 24–30 s).
- **Look:** white ground (#ffffff), ink #171716, one gold #f9b233 used for the bar's second segment, the send button and the dot; Hanken Grotesk 700 for the number and lines, the app's own type inside the phone; no gradients, no glow.
- **Sound:** a warm, steady 100 bpm bed that stays quiet during the number, a soft tick as the bar fills, a real-sounding click on each tap, one riser into a single impact when "Enviado." lands, then the bed resolves under the end card. Silent-first: every line is on screen as large type.

## B — "Antes e depois de segunda-feira"

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
**World:** two halves of the frame at the start; the left half is the old Monday, the right half is the same Monday in the app.

- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the left half is a paper notebook of unpaid names being crossed out, a spreadsheet with red rows, a message typed by hand and deleted twice (all drawn as flat shapes, because the old way has no source to show). The right half is the same debt in Cobrar: the reminder already written. The left half churns; the right half is calm. They collapse into the right half. The feeling is recognition, then ease.
- **First two seconds, exactly:** frame 0 is the two halves already running: a hand striking a name out of a notebook on the left, the Cobrar list on the right with "378 000 Kz" in both. At 1.0 s the left half's counter ticks up "dia 49, dia 50, dia 51" while the right half stays still.
- **Beats:** hook (split, 0–2 s) → tension (the left half fails: the unsent message, 2–8 s) → reveal (the right half named, 8 s) → proof (the same debt, in the app, 8–18 s) → payoff (the left half collapses, the right fills the frame, "Enviado.", 18–22 s) → claim → end card (certificate and "Abrir conta grátis", 24–30 s).
- **Look:** two grounds at once: warm grey (#e7e5e4) with ink line drawings on the left, white with the real app on the right; the same ink and gold; type is Hanken Grotesk 700.
- **Sound:** the left half has paper and a hesitant keyboard, almost no music; when the right half takes over the bed opens up at 110 bpm. Clicks on taps, one impact at the collapse.

## C — "Tire o til" (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** match cuts: one shape holds while everything around it changes.
**World:** colour fields that change every beat, with the phone appearing only inside them; the gold dot is the thing that never changes.

- **What the viewer sees and feels:** it is the page's own joke made into a film. "Facturação" loses its til and its cedilha and becomes "facturac.ao". The gold dot then becomes the segment of the share bar, the segment becomes the send button, the button becomes the shield on the certificate, and the shield becomes the dot of the web address you type to open an account. Four match cuts, one gold circle. The feeling is wit and confidence.
- **First two seconds, exactly:** frame 0 is "Facturação" at 230 px, ink on white, with a gold dot hovering over the "ç". At 0.5 s the til lifts off the "ã" and flies out of frame, at 1.1 s the cedilla drops; at 1.8 s the dot lands between "c" and "ao".
- **Beats:** hook (the type, 0–3 s) → tension ("atenc.ao": 1 de janeiro de 2027, on an ink field, 3–7 s) → reveal ("facturac.ao" named, 7 s) → proof (the dot becomes the bar, the bar the button, the button sends "Enviado.", 7–19 s) → payoff (the dot becomes the shield: Certificada pela AGT, 19–23 s) → claim → end card (the dot becomes the full stop of "Abrir conta grátis", 25–30 s).
- **Look:** full colour fields (white, ink #171716, gold #f9b233) at hard edges, no gradients; Hanken Grotesk 700 at giant sizes; the real app appears at 1.6–2× its real size inside the fields, never floating small on an empty canvas.
- **Sound:** beat-led: a tight 120 bpm bed with the cuts on the beat, one tick per match cut, a click on the send, a riser into one impact at the shield, bed resolves on the end-card full stop.

## Assumptions (change any of them)

- **Length 28–32 s, 1080×1920 at 30 fps**, built for WhatsApp status and Reels. Everything the viewer must read sits inside the platform band (y from 168 to 1330 px); below 1330 px only texture and the platform's own captions and buttons.
- **No voice-over, music and effects** (free: the HeyGen library track already in `videos/facturac-ao-cobrar/assets/bgm`, which is a different feel from the newer catalog; the free option is that file). If you want a voice, the continuous ElevenLabs read from the earlier teasers is the option that suits this audience (paid per read).
- **Burned-in captions** are not needed: the big type carries every line, so the film works muted.
- **Real app screens**: captured fresh from the app at 3x (Hoje, Cobrar, the reminder panel, the send state). I add the "Enviado." state by capturing it from a real send in the local demo, with the mail faked, so no email leaves.
- No customer names, quotes or statistics, because none have been supplied.
